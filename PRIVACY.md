# Privacy policy

GistDroid is an open-source GitHub Gist client. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Your GitHub personal access token**, encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and your GitHub
  username and avatar URL.
- **Local snippets** you create or save, in the app's private database.
- **Settings** such as theme, color palette and text size.

This data stays in the app's private storage. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for
the app, and everything is deleted when you uninstall it. Signing out deletes the token and its key.

## Network access

The app only connects to GitHub, over HTTPS:

- `api.github.com`, with your token, to read and change your gists;
- GitHub's file and avatar hosts, to download gist file contents and your avatar.

What GitHub does with this data is covered by the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## No tracking

GistDroid contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other third-party SDKs.

## Files you choose

When you import, upload, download, share or back up files, the app only reads or writes the files you pick in
the system file picker or share sheet.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are recorded in this file's history in the
[GistDroid repository](https://github.com/iamgiven/GistDroid).
